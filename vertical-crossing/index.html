How to play

Both views share exactly the same vertical scale. Multiple heights at one time produce multiple objects.

Use Erase and drag over part of a drawing to remove it. Undo restores the previous drawing change.

Draw with Freehand, Line, or Point. Press Play or Pause below the graph.

Drag the orange handle below the graph left or right to inspect a particular moment or move backward. Use the Step buttons for smaller changes. The barriers move with time.

Motorcycle: stay inside the road and reach the finish before the timer expires. Potholes and mud slow the bike. Frogger: avoid traffic moving in both directions. Basketball: draw at least two bounces and join the release point at six seconds. Drag that point vertically to change launch height. The hand follows the ball within reach; hand contact is not tested. Leaving the ball on the floor too long is a double-dribble foul in this challenge. Beyond the divider, physics draws the flight, using the final graph slope as launch velocity.

A collision stops every object and marks the contact with a red ×. Rewind to inspect or change your drawing. Courses stay fixed until you choose New course.

After the shot is released, the camera follows the ball; the player stays at his shooting spot on the floor and scrolls off left. The hoop enters near the end. Rewinding restores the player, ball, court, and basket to the same moment.
