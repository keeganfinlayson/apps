How to play

Both views share exactly the same vertical scale. Multiple heights at one time produce multiple objects.

Use Erase and drag over part of a drawing to remove it. Undo restores the previous drawing change.

Draw with Freehand, Line, or Point. Press Play; press Pause to stop.

Drag the orange time line, choose Move time and drag anywhere on the graph, or use the time slider. Arrow keys on the slider move by 0.01 seconds.

Motorcycle: avoid potholes. Frog: avoid cars. Basketball: draw a bounce at the floor, then lift the ball into the green net opening. Orange blocks are barriers.

A collision stops every object and marks the contact with a red ×. Rewind to inspect or change your drawing. Courses stay fixed until you choose New course.

Each height at the same time creates another object. Any object can hit a barrier.
